## Why Her Family Rejected the Murder Theory

As Amy Eskridge’s name became attached to broader claims about allegedly suspicious deaths among scientists and researchers, family members publicly challenged the suggestion that she had been killed because of what she knew.

The clearest reported statement came from her father, Richard Eskridge, a retired NASA-affiliated engineer and co-founder of some of the ventures associated with Amy’s research activities. In interviews and later summaries of the controversy, he dismissed claims of a hidden conspiracy and argued that there was no basis for treating her death as evidence of a wider plot. In accounts discussing the resurgence of the case in 2026, he was portrayed as rejecting the idea that Amy had been targeted by a secret organisation, intelligence service, or anti-disclosure network.
