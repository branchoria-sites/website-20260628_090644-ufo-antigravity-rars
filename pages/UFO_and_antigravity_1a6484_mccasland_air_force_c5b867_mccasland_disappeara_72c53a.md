## Introduction

William “Neil” McCasland’s disappearance on 27 February 2026 became the focal point of intense speculation because of his distinguished Air Force career and later associations with figures involved in UFO discussions. Yet the actual timeline of his disappearance is striking enough without invoking classified-secrets theories. What is firmly established is that the retired major general vanished from his Albuquerque home within a relatively short window of time, leaving behind several personal items but taking others, and that extensive searches failed to locate him. Months later, the case remained unresolved despite the involvement of local, state and federal agencies. The evidence paints a picture of a puzzling missing-person investigation rather than a proven conspiracy.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cbsnews.com/news/retired-air-force-major-general-missing-new-mexico-william-neil-mccasland/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cbsnews.com">[CBS News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cbsnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">CBS News Retired Air Force major general still missing 2 weeks after</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">CBS News Retired Air Force major general still missing 2 weeks after</span></span></span>

## The First Days of the Search

The day after the disappearance, authorities issued a Silver Alert, reflecting concern that medical factors could have placed McCasland at risk. Officials publicly stated that he had an unspecified medical issue, adding urgency to search operations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://abc7.com/post/william-neil-mccasland-missing-retired-us-air-force-major-general-commanded-base-long-associated-ufo-lore/18707800/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: abc7.com">[ABC7 Los Angeles]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">abc7.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Los Angeles William Neil Mc Casland missing: Retired US Air Force maj</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">16 Mar 2026 — William Neil McCasland, 68, left his Albuquerque home on foot at approximately 11 a.m. February 27 and has not been in cont...</span></span></span>
